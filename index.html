Inspection Checklist

SIX SIGNS YOUR WATER HEATER IS ON ITS WAY OUT

If two or more of these sound familiar, it's worth a look before it fails on you completely — usually at the worst possible time.

01

It's more than 10 years old (check the sticker on the tank).

02

Water coming out with a rust or brown tint.

03

Popping, rumbling, or banging sounds from inside the tank.

04

Water pooling on the floor around the base.

05

Hot water runs out faster than it used to.

06

Pilot light won't stay lit, or the breaker keeps tripping.
